Everything you need to organise and enjoy your local videos.

LocalStream focuses on the daily work of making personal video folders easier to browse, organise, resume, maintain, and move to another computer without starting over.

Take your library setup with you

Create a LocalStream backup, restore your supported library organisation on another computer, and avoid rebuilding your setup from scratch after moving PCs or reinstalling.

LocalStream backups contain supported LocalStream library information, not your original video files. Your original media still needs to be copied, connected, or made available separately.
