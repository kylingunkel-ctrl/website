About LocalStream

LocalStream is for the videos you already have.

Personal video collections grow over time. LocalStream turns folders and drives full of videos into a clean, visual library you can browse, organise and enjoy.

Your videos should not disappear into folders.

Most personal video libraries are built slowly. Family clips, travel footage, archived recordings, personal projects, and saved media end up spread across folders, external drives, old laptops, exports, and backup archives.

Windows folders are useful, but they are not designed to make a large video collection feel enjoyable to browse. Over time, people forget what they already have. LocalStream exists to make those collections feel organised, visual, and easy to return to again.

Built for real personal collections

  • Family videos
  • Home movies
  • Travel footage
  • Old digital recordings
  • Personal projects
  • Archived footage
  • Owned or downloaded media
  • Collections on external drives

LocalStream is designed for people who already have a collection they care about and want to make it easier to browse. It works best when the goal is not to stream from an online catalogue, but to rediscover, organise, and enjoy videos you already store yourself.

What makes LocalStream different

Your files stay yours

LocalStream works with media already stored on your computer or connected drives. It does not require you to move your library into a separate cloud platform.

Built around your own collection

No online streaming catalogue is required. LocalStream is there to help you make sense of the videos you already keep.

Visual, not just folders

Thumbnails, search, tags, playlists, favourites, and progress make large collections easier to browse than plain file listings.

No need to rebuild your media

LocalStream organises existing files rather than asking you to rebuild your collection inside another service.

Back up your library setup

Where supported, LocalStream can back up library organisation data so your setup can be restored on another computer. Original video files remain separate from that backup.

Private by default

The focus stays on local playback and local organisation, with users keeping control over where their videos live and how their library is arranged.

Product principles

Private by design

Your personal collection should not need a cloud media library just to feel organised.

Simple to understand

Choose folders, browse your videos, organise what matters, and come back to it later without complexity.

Built for local media

LocalStream is shaped around folders, drives, archives, and personal libraries that already exist.

Polished enough to enjoy

A local video library should feel calm, legible, and satisfying to use, not just technically functional.

Your collection stays under your control

You decide where your files live, what gets connected, and how your library is organised.

What LocalStream is not

Not a streaming service

LocalStream does not offer a subscription video catalogue.

Not a cloud storage provider

It is not designed to replace your own storage, backup routine, or file ownership.

Not a media server

The focus is a private Windows desktop experience for browsing and watching your own local files.

Not a source of copyrighted content

LocalStream helps organise media you already lawfully store. It does not provide movies, shows, or copyrighted downloads.
