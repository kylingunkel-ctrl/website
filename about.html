About

LocalStream is for the videos you already have.

Personal collections often end up scattered across folders, drives, exports, and old projects. LocalStream gives those files a private desktop library without pretending to be a cloud service.

Principles

LocalStream values privacy, simplicity, careful organisation, and a premium interface: calm, fast, legible, and respectful of the user.

What it is not

LocalStream is not a subscription streaming platform, a source of video content, or a media server. It helps people browse and manage local videos they already store.
