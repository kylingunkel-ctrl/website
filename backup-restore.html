Backup & Restore

Take your library setup with you.

Back up your supported LocalStream library and organisation data, then restore it on another computer so you do not have to rebuild your setup from scratch.

Your videos are separate from your LocalStream backup

LocalStream Backup & Restore protects supported LocalStream library and organisation information. It does not copy your original video files. Keep your own backup of important videos and make sure those files are available on the computer where you restore LocalStream.

Moving LocalStream to a new computer

Changing computers does not mean you need to organise your LocalStream library from scratch. Create a LocalStream backup on your old computer, restore it on the new one, then reconnect the folders or drives containing your original videos.

1. Create a backup

Create a LocalStream backup on your old computer before you move, replace, or reinstall anything.

2. Make your videos available

Copy your original video files to the new computer, or connect the same external drive or storage that already contains them.

3. Install LocalStream

Install LocalStream on the new computer you want to use.

4. Restore the backup

Restore your LocalStream backup so the supported library setup returns.

5. Reconnect folders or drives

If folder paths or drive letters changed, reselect or reconnect the relevant video locations in LocalStream.

6. Check your setup

Confirm your supported library organisation has returned and that your original videos are available where LocalStream expects them.

What a LocalStream backup is for

Use LocalStream backups to protect supported LocalStream library information such as organisation and viewing data. Current website and project documentation describes this broadly as supported library information, including items such as tags, playlists, favourites, custom titles, cover art, thumbnails, settings, and watch status.

Use your own storage backup routine for the original video files themselves.

Folder and drive changes after restoring

When you move to another computer, the storage path may not look exactly the same as before. External drives may receive a different drive letter, folders may live in a different location, and LocalStream may need you to point it back to the correct place.

If your backup restores but videos appear missing, first check whether the original files are present in File Explorer and whether the correct folder or drive is connected.

When this is useful

Moving to a new Windows PC

Keep your supported LocalStream setup without rebuilding it video by video.

Reinstalling Windows or LocalStream

Restore your supported organisation after a fresh install.

Recovering after a hardware change

Bring your LocalStream setup back after replacing or repairing a computer.

Privacy note

Backup files may describe your personal library structure. Store them somewhere you trust.
