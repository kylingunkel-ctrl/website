Backup & Restore

Take your library setup with you.

Back up your supported FolderStream library and organisation data, then restore it on another computer so you do not have to rebuild your setup from scratch.

Your videos are separate from your FolderStream backup

FolderStream Backup & Restore protects supported FolderStream library and organisation information. It does not copy your original video files. Keep your own backup of important videos and make sure those files are available on the computer where you restore FolderStream.

Moving FolderStream to a new computer

Changing computers does not mean you need to organise your FolderStream library from scratch. Create a FolderStream backup on your old computer, restore it on the new one, then reconnect the folders or drives containing your original videos.

1. Create a backup

Create a FolderStream backup on your old computer before you move, replace, or reinstall anything.

2. Make your videos available

Copy your original video files to the new computer, or connect the same external drive or storage that already contains them.

3. Install FolderStream

Install FolderStream on the new computer you want to use.

4. Restore the backup

Restore your FolderStream backup so the supported library setup returns.

5. Reconnect folders or drives

If folder paths or drive letters changed, reselect or reconnect the relevant video locations in FolderStream.

6. Check your setup

Confirm your supported library organisation has returned and that your original videos are available where FolderStream expects them.

What a FolderStream backup is for

Use FolderStream backups to protect supported FolderStream library information such as organisation and viewing data. Current website and project documentation describes this broadly as supported library information, including items such as tags, playlists, favourites, custom titles, cover art, thumbnails, settings, and watch status.

Use your own storage backup routine for the original video files themselves.

Folder and drive changes after restoring

When you move to another computer, the storage path may not look exactly the same as before. External drives may receive a different drive letter, folders may live in a different location, and FolderStream may need you to point it back to the correct place.

If your backup restores but videos appear missing, first check whether the original files are present in File Explorer and whether the correct folder or drive is connected.

When this is useful

Moving to a new Windows PC

Keep your supported FolderStream setup without rebuilding it video by video.

Reinstalling Windows or FolderStream

Restore your supported organisation after a fresh install.

Recovering after a hardware change

Bring your FolderStream setup back after replacing or repairing a computer.

Privacy note

Backup files may describe your personal library structure. Store them somewhere you trust.
