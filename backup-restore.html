Backup & Restore

Keep your organisation data portable.

Backups are for LocalStream library information such as tags, playlists, favourites, titles, cover art, thumbnails, settings, and watch status. Your video files remain in your own folders.

What to back up

Use LocalStream backup tools for local metadata and organisation data. Keep separate backups of the actual video files using your preferred storage routine.

Restoring

Restore organisation data after reinstalling, changing devices, or rebuilding a library. Reconnect the same video folders or drives so LocalStream can match saved data to local files.

Privacy note

Backup files may describe your personal library structure. Store them somewhere you trust.
