Privacy Policy for FolderStream for Android

FolderStream for Android reads videos from folders you select through Android’s file picker. It uses those permissions to index and play your videos and create thumbnails.

The app keeps folder references, video metadata, thumbnails, playback progress, favourites, tags, playlists and settings on your device. FolderStream does not collect or share this data or send your videos to FolderStream servers. The Android app has no account, advertising or analytics.

People processes frames from your videos on your device to find and group faces. FolderStream stores face images, numerical face descriptors, profile names and video associations locally. You can turn off People scanning or clear its data in FolderStream Settings.

If you export a library backup, FolderStream saves library records and settings to the destination you choose. Portable backups can also include People profiles, images, face descriptors and video associations. Video files are excluded. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the backup under its own privacy policy.

You can remove indexed folders in Settings and delete app data through Android’s settings or by uninstalling the app. Exported backups remain where you saved them until you delete them.

For privacy questions, contact support@folderstream.co.nz.

FolderStream Support uses the information you choose to send to respond to your request. For details about support correspondence and visits to this website, see the FolderStream website and desktop privacy policy.
