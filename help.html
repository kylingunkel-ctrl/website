Getting started

Choose a folder that contains your videos. LocalStream reads that location and builds a visual library view around the files already stored on your computer or connected drives.

Once your library appears, you can browse thumbnails, use search and filters, save watch progress, and organise videos with tags, playlists, favourites, custom titles, and custom cover art.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for common tasks.

Can I move my LocalStream library to another computer?

Yes. You can create a LocalStream backup and restore your supported library organisation on another computer. Your original video files are not included in that backup, so they must also be copied, connected, or otherwise available on the new computer. If folder or drive locations change, you may need to reconnect them in LocalStream.

Does LocalStream copy or upload my videos?

No. LocalStream is designed around local folders and connected drives selected by you. It does not upload your videos to a cloud service.

What does a LocalStream backup include?

LocalStream backups are for supported LocalStream library information and organisation data, not the original video files themselves.

Can I use videos stored on an external drive?

Yes. LocalStream can work with user-selected folders on connected drives. The drive must be connected for those video files to play.

Troubleshooting

Start with the most common causes.

I restored my backup on another computer but LocalStream can’t find my videos

What you’re seeing: your tags, playlists, or other LocalStream organisation may have restored, but videos are unavailable or marked as missing.

Why it may happen: the original video files were not copied to the new computer, the external drive is not connected, the drive letter changed, the folder path changed, or the videos were moved somewhere else.

What to try: confirm the original files exist in File Explorer, connect the external drive if needed, check the current folder or drive location, then reconnect or reselect the relevant library folder in LocalStream.

Still stuck? Contact support@localstream.co.nz.

A connected drive is missing

If videos live on an external drive, reconnect that drive before trying to play them. Library organisation may still appear even while the original files are unavailable.

I need backup guidance

See the full Backup & Restore guide for moving LocalStream to another computer, reinstalling, or restoring after a system problem.

Contact support

If you are still stuck, use the Support page to prepare a local support report and describe what happened. If you are troubleshooting a moved or restored library, include whether the original videos are on the same drive, a new drive, or a copied folder on the new computer.
